# Homepage & Navbar Reference Analysis — Dularhat Secondary School

## 1. Visual Strategy & Design Philosophy

The new homepage and navigation design elevate Dularhat Secondary School into a bespoke, editorial, institutional digital presence that celebrates its heritage (Est. 1963/1966, EIIN 101297, Charfashion, Bhola) while delivering an ultra-modern user experience.

---

## 2. Reference 1: Hero & Visual Composition Analysis

### Key Principles:
- **Large Editorial Display**: Prominent Bengali display typography for `দুলারহাট মাধ্যমিক বিদ্যালয়` paired with refined subheadings (`চরফ্যাশন, ভোলা`).
- **Architectural Image Integration**: The official school photograph (`school-photo-main.jpg` featuring the multi-storey campus, Shaheed Minar, and green grounds) is incorporated as an architectural centerpiece with elegant framing, subtle atmospheric depth, and backdrop layering.
- **Controlled Palette**: Deep institutional heritage green (`#014D00`, `#013500`), warm gold accents (`#F5B738`, `#B8892A`), crisp white, and warm off-white tones (`#FAFAF7`, `#F5F3EE`).
- **Balanced Spacing & Whitespace**: Generous vertical padding, clear typographic rhythm, and strong asymmetric layout that avoids repetitive card grids.
- **Direct Action Hierarchy**: High-contrast primary action (`বিদ্যালয় সম্পর্কে`) + transparent glass secondary action (`সর্বশেষ নোটিশ`).

---

## 3. Reference 2: Navbar Transformation & Architecture

### Key Principles:
- **Scroll-Adaptive Behavior**:
  - **Top State**: Atmospheric, integrated seamlessly with the hero section, generous vertical breathing room, transparent or glass backdrop.
  - **Scrolled State**: Compact, opaque solid forest green (`#013D00` / `#013000`), subtle bottom border/shadow, compact logo, perfectly sticky.
- **Logo-First Identity**: Uses the circular official emblem (`logo.jpg`). The navbar does *not* occupy valuable horizontal space with long full school titles; instead it focuses on clean navigation links with a compact badge or logo alone.
- **Refined Navigation Structure**:
  - হোম
  - আমাদের সম্পর্কে ▾ (বিদ্যালয় পরিচিতি, ইতিহাস, লক্ষ্য ও উদ্দেশ্য)
  - প্রশাসন ▾ (প্রধান শিক্ষক, পরিচালনা কমিটি, শিক্ষকবৃন্দ, কর্মচারীবৃন্দ)
  - একাডেমিক ▾ (শ্রেণি ও বিষয়, ক্লাস রুটিন, পরীক্ষার তথ্য)
  - ভর্তি
  - নোটিশ
  - ফলাফল
  - গ্যালারি
  - যোগাযোগ
- **Mobile First**: Smooth full-screen or sliding drawer with clear tap targets, language switch, and contact access.

---

## 4. Below-the-Fold Editorial Structure

1. **Latest Notices Strip**: Editorial date stamps, category indicators, priority badges, and quick PDF download actions.
2. **School Narrative & History**: Editorial typography, large historical milestone year typography (`1963`), and founder/first headmaster chronicle.
3. **Key Statistics**: High-impact typographic numbers with minimal chrome.
4. **Headmaster's Address**: Editorial portrait/quote layout on warm backing.
5. **Academic Disciplines**: Science, Business Studies, Humanities in a unified layout.
6. **Campus & Life Gallery**: Real school photographs in an asymmetric visual presentation.
7. **Direct Services / Quick Links**: Compact, high-utility directory.
8. **Contact & Location**: Verified address, phone numbers, email, and Google Maps lookup.
